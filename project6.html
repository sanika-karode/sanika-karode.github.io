Face Time Phone Stand

A common issue faced during video calls is the inability to have the phone charging without having to try to balance it on various items or flip the phone sideways or upside down, causing deformed video capture. To address this, I plan to design the "Face Time Phone Stand," a 3D-printed accessory that aligns the phone's camera with the user's line of sight. This project involved CAD modeling, prototyping, and user testing to ensure comfort and functionality. Currently I am in the sketching phase and will have a CAD design posted soon!
